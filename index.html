About

Named for the mud.

Pluff mud is the soft, dark, sun-warmed mud of the South Carolina salt marsh. Anyone who's waded a Lowcountry creek at low tide knows it, and its smell, right away.

Pluff Games LLC is an independent studio with Charleston-marsh roots. We design, build and ship our own games end to end, at our own pace.

We like games that are easy to pick up and hard to put down: clear rules, a good feel, and fair play when it comes to your wallet.
