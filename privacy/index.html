Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 13, 2026

This policy covers this website (pluff.games) and the general practices of Pluff Games LLC ("we"). Each game has its own policy with game-specific details:

  • Needle vs Wedge: see the privacy page on needlevswedge.com
  • Rackstar: rackstar.pluff.games/privacy

This website

pluff.games is a static site. It has no accounts, forms or cookies. It uses Cloudflare Web Analytics, which is cookieless and collects aggregate visit data like page views, referrer, country and device type, with no personal profiles or cross-site tracking. It is hosted on GitHub Pages, which may log basic technical data such as IP addresses for security; see GitHub's privacy statement.

Our games

We don't run accounts in our games, and we don't collect your name, email or contacts through them. Some games use third-party services such as Google AdMob, Apple Game Center or web game portals, which handle data under their own policies, as described in each game's policy.

Email

If you email us, we use your message and address only to reply.

Children

Our games are not directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect their personal information.
